# Private Residence in Natural Setting

This contemporary villa sits on a hillside site overlooking Sibiu, designed as a private residence that balances modern comfort with respect for the natural landscape. The 350 square meter home creates intimate connections between interior living spaces and the surrounding environment.

## Site and Context

The sloping site presented both challenges and opportunities. Rather than flattening the terrain, the design works with the natural topography, minimizing site disturbance and creating multiple levels that follow the hillside contours. This approach reduces construction impact while offering varied perspectives of the valley below.

Design priorities included:

- Preserving existing trees and natural vegetation
- Maximizing views while maintaining privacy
- Creating seamless indoor-outdoor connections
- Using sustainable materials and construction methods
- Integrating the building harmoniously into the landscape

## Architectural Concept

The villa's form responds to site conditions and programmatic needs. A linear plan follows the hillside contour, with living spaces oriented to capture southern light and valley views. Large sliding glass panels dissolve boundaries between interior and exterior, extending living areas onto terraces that serve as outdoor rooms.

The material palette emphasizes natural textures—locally sourced stone, natural wood cladding, and smooth plaster surfaces create a restrained aesthetic that complements the landscape. A green roof on the lower level blends the structure into the hillside when viewed from above.

## Interior Spaces

Interior layouts emphasize spatial continuity and natural light. The entry level contains living, dining, and kitchen areas configured as a flowing open space. Full-height glazing provides constant visual connection to the landscape. A double-height space at the living area creates dramatic volume while facilitating natural ventilation.

The lower level includes three bedrooms, each with direct access to private terrace areas. The master suite opens onto a secluded garden carved into the hillside. A home office occupies a quiet corner with views through the tree canopy.

![Interior detail](./images/gallery-04.jpg)

## Sustainable Features

Environmental performance was integral to the design:

- High-performance envelope minimizes heating and cooling loads
- Geothermal heat pump provides efficient climate control
- Solar panels integrated into the roof structure
- Natural ventilation strategies reduce mechanical cooling needs
- Rainwater collection for landscape irrigation
- Native plantings require minimal water and maintenance

## Materials and Details

Material selection prioritized durability, low maintenance, and environmental responsibility. Interior floors use large-format porcelain tiles and natural oak. Built-in furniture maximizes space efficiency while reducing material use. Kitchen and bathroom fixtures balance aesthetic quality with water conservation.

## Outdoor Living

Landscape design extends the architectural concept. Multiple terrace levels create distinct outdoor rooms—from the main entertaining terrace off the living area to intimate seating areas integrated into the garden. Indigenous plantings attract local wildlife while requiring minimal irrigation.

## Project Completion

Construction was completed in September 2023. The villa demonstrates that contemporary residential architecture can achieve high performance and modern comfort while respecting natural settings and minimizing environmental impact. The owners report high satisfaction with the quality of living spaces and the building's energy performance.

This project illustrates our belief that thoughtful residential design creates homes that enhance daily life while treading lightly on the landscape.
