# Next-Generation Logistics Facility

The Industrial Logistics Hub in Cluj-Napoca represents a state-of-the-art warehouse and distribution center designed for modern supply chain operations. Spanning 25,000 square meters, this facility will serve as a regional logistics center for multiple international clients.

## Project Overview

This industrial complex is being developed to meet the growing demand for high-quality logistics infrastructure in Transylvania. The design emphasizes operational efficiency, flexibility, and environmental sustainability.

Primary project goals:

- Create a flexible warehouse space adaptable to various logistics operations
- Implement sustainable building practices and renewable energy systems
- Ensure efficient traffic flow for heavy vehicles
- Provide modern office facilities for operational staff
- Meet BREEAM Excellent certification standards

## Architectural Design

The warehouse features a clear-span structure that maximizes usable floor space without interior columns. This open layout allows clients to configure the space according to their specific operational needs. The building envelope uses high-performance insulated panels that maintain stable interior temperatures year-round.

The facility includes a two-story office wing attached to the main warehouse, providing 2,000 square meters of administrative space with natural lighting and modern amenities. Large loading docks with adjustable height platforms accommodate various vehicle types.

## Sustainable Infrastructure

Environmental considerations are central to this project:

- 4,000 square meter solar panel array on the roof
- LED lighting throughout with motion sensors
- Rainwater harvesting system for facility operations
- Electric vehicle charging infrastructure
- Green buffer zones with native vegetation

## Construction Progress

Currently in the structural phase, the steel framework has been completed and exterior wall installation is underway. The project is scheduled for completion in late 2024. Once operational, the facility will create approximately 150 jobs in the region.

![Aerial construction view](./images/gallery-04.jpg)

## Technical Specifications

The warehouse floor is designed to support heavy racking systems with a load capacity of 5 tons per square meter. The clear height of 12 meters allows for maximum vertical storage efficiency. Fire suppression systems, security infrastructure, and building management systems are being integrated throughout the facility.

## Future Development

The site includes provisions for future expansion, with an additional 10,000 square meters available for development as client demand grows. This phased approach allows for flexible growth while managing capital investment efficiently.

This logistics hub will strengthen Cluj-Napoca's position as a key distribution point in Central Europe, supporting regional economic development and providing modern infrastructure for international logistics operations.
