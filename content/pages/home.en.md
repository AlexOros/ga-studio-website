## About GA Studio

GA Studio is an architecture and engineering practice based in Romania, serving clients throughout Central and Eastern Europe. Since our founding, we have completed diverse projects spanning residential, commercial, industrial, and urban planning sectors.

Our multidisciplinary team brings together architects, structural engineers, and urban planners who share a commitment to design excellence and technical rigor. We approach each project as a unique opportunity to create spaces that respond to specific needs while contributing positively to their context.

We are committed to sustainable design practices that reduce environmental impact and create healthier, more efficient buildings. Our work demonstrates that environmental responsibility and design quality are complementary objectives.
