# Modern Living in Historic Alba Iulia

This contemporary residential complex represents a harmonious blend of modern architecture and the historic character of Alba Iulia. The project encompasses 120 residential units distributed across four buildings, designed to maximize natural light and create a sense of community.

## Design Philosophy

Our approach focused on creating living spaces that respond to contemporary needs while respecting the urban context. The buildings feature clean lines, large windows, and sustainable materials that reduce environmental impact.

Key design principles included:

- Maximizing natural light in all living spaces
- Creating private outdoor spaces for each unit
- Incorporating green spaces throughout the complex
- Using energy-efficient materials and systems
- Ensuring accessibility for all residents

## Architectural Features

The complex showcases several innovative architectural solutions. Each building is oriented to optimize solar exposure, reducing heating costs while providing residents with abundant natural light. The facades combine high-quality materials including textured concrete, aluminum panels, and large-format glazing.

Ground floor units feature private gardens, while upper-floor apartments include generous terraces. The communal areas incorporate native landscaping that requires minimal maintenance and supports local biodiversity.

## Sustainability Features

Environmental responsibility guided every decision in this project:

- High-performance insulation exceeding national standards
- Energy-efficient heating and cooling systems
- Rainwater collection for irrigation
- Bike storage and electric vehicle charging stations
- Native plant species requiring minimal water

## Community Spaces

The complex includes carefully designed communal areas that encourage social interaction. A central courtyard serves as a gathering space, featuring seating areas, play structures for children, and walking paths. Underground parking preserves the ground level for green space and recreation.

![Interior courtyard](./images/gallery-04.svg)

## Project Details

The construction utilized modern building techniques to ensure structural integrity and energy efficiency. Each residential unit features high-quality finishes, flexible floor plans, and modern amenities. The project was completed on schedule and has been fully occupied since summer 2023.

This residential complex demonstrates that contemporary architecture can enhance urban environments while providing comfortable, sustainable living spaces for families. The project has received positive feedback from residents and has set a new standard for residential development in Alba Iulia.
